## 2. Trade-off memo

> **Rock #1:** 4 Fix the notification system (wrong times, timezone bug), connects to KR 3 because fixing notification delivery increases the likelihood that post-acute users re-engage with Fable during relevant life moments, directly supporting our successful re-engagement.
>
> **Rock #2:** 6 Add AI-generated daily check-in prompts personalised to user history, connects to KR 1, 2 and 3 because personalised check-ins use a user's wellness history to increase confidence in handling future stress (KR1), encourage proactive action when early warning signs appear (KR2), and create a compelling reason for post-acute users to return to Fable during future stressful moments (KR3).
>
> **Rock #3:** 1 Redesign the onboarding flow based on 40% drop-off data connects to KR 1 and KR 3 because a better onboarding experience helps users understand the value of Fable beyond the acute phase, increasing confidence in managing future stress (KR1) and increasing the likelihood that users stay long enough to return and re-engage when they need support later (KR3).
>
> **Hard No #1:** 2 Add a daily streak feature to drive habit formation Avoid because it optimizes for app usage rather than resilience and does not directly improve KR1 (confidence), KR2 (proactive action), or KR3 (successful re-engagement) this quarter.
>
> **Hard No #2:** 12 Build a content library (guided meditations, articles, audio), Avoid because it moves Fable into a crowded content market and does not validate our core hypothesis that personalized wellness memory improves post-acute retention.
>
> **Hard No #3:** 13 Introduce "Fable for Teams", a B2B workplace wellbeing play, Avoid because it introduces a new customer, buyer, and business model when our priority this quarter is proving retention and re-engagement for post-acute individual users.
