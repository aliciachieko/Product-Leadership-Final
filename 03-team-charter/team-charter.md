## 1. What We Own

| Area | We own it | We influence it (don't own) |
|---|---|---|
| Post-Acute Retention Strategy | Strategy, roadmap, and OKRs for retaining users after their acute need is resolved (KR1, KR2, KR3) | Growth experiments, lifecycle channels, clinical recommendations, acquisition goals, content strategy, B2B strategy |
| Onboarding (PM1) | Activation, onboarding experience, onboarding conversion, onboarding drop-off | Personalization inputs collected during onboarding, future retention outcomes, AI recommendation logic, retention programs |
| Retention (PM2) | Re-engagement journeys, lifecycle experiences, notification strategy, post-acute retention outcomes | Onboarding messaging, AI-powered recommendations used in retention flows, AI model design, onboarding activation metrics |
| AI Check-ins (PM3) | Personalization engine, wellness memory, recommendation quality, AI-generated check-ins | Onboarding experiences that use AI, retention campaigns that use AI, notification delivery strategy, onboarding conversion targets |
| Out of scope | To maintain focus on our strategy of improving post-acute retention through confidence, proactive action, and successful re-engagement, the following are out of scope for this team: Growth initiatives primarily focused on increasing usage metrics such as DAU, session frequency, streaks, or time spent in app. Content platform investments including large libraries of meditations, articles, or audio content as a primary growth strategy. Clinical care delivery including therapist networks, care provision, diagnosis, or treatment services. B2B and employer wellness products such as Fable for Teams. New market expansion (new geographies, languages, or customer segments) unless explicitly tied to validating or scaling the post-acute retention model. Platform expansion for its own sake (web, wearables, integrations) without evidence that it improves KR1, KR2, or KR3. Acquisition-focused growth work that does not contribute to confidence, proactive intervention, or successful re-engagement outcomes. Features designed primarily to drive habit formation or dependency, including streak mechanics, social pressure loops, or other engagement-first patterns. |  |
| Joint call: Any AI experience introduced in onboarding | Joint call between PM1 (Onboarding) and PM3 (AI Check-ins) because it impacts activation, user understanding, and personalization. |  |
| Joint call: Any AI experience used for retention or re-engagement | Joint call between PM2 (Retention) and PM3 (AI Check-ins) because it affects return behavior, notification strategy, and KR3. |  |
| Joint call: Changes to notification strategy, frequency, timing, or targeting | Joint call between PM2 (Retention) and PM3 (AI Check-ins) because outreach is both a retention mechanism and a personalization mechanism. |  |
| Joint call: Changes to user data collection, wellness memory, or personalization rules | Joint call between PM1, PM2, and PM3 because those decisions influence onboarding, AI relevance, and long-term retention. |  |
| Joint call: Any initiative expected to move more than one team's primary KPI or OKR | Joint call between affected PMs because no single team should optimize its own metric at the expense of overall customer outcomes. |  |
| Joint call: Roadmap prioritization decisions involving trade-offs between activation, retention, and personalization | Joint call between PM1, PM2, PM3, and the Core Product Lead because these decisions affect strategy execution across the entire post-acute journey. |  |

## 2. How We Decide

| Decision type | Who decides | Who's consulted | How we break a tie |
|---|---|---|---|
| Onboarding Experience Changes | PM1 (Onboarding) | PM3 if AI is involved; Growth if acquisition or activation metrics are affected | The option that best improves activation while supporting long-term post-acute retention wins |
| Retention and Re-engagement Experiences | PM2 (Retention) | PM3 if personalization is involved; Growth if lifecycle campaigns are affected | The option that best improves KR3 (successful re-engagement) without creating user dependency wins |
| AI Check-In Logic, Wellness Memory, and Recommendation Quality | PM3 (AI Check-ins) | PM1 and PM2 if the AI experience appears in onboarding or retention surfaces | The option that best improves KR1 (confidence) and KR2 (proactive action) wins |
| AI Experiences Introduced During Onboarding | PM1 (Onboarding), as the owner of the customer-facing experience | PM3 (AI Check-ins) | The option that best improves onboarding activation while preserving future personalization value and long-term retention wins |
| AI Experiences Used for Retention or Re-engagement | PM2 (Retention), as the owner of the customer-facing experience | PM3 (AI Check-ins) | The option that best improves KR3 (successful re-engagement) wins |
| Notification Strategy, Timing, Frequency, and Targeting | PM2 (Retention) | PM3 (AI Check-ins), Engineering, and Growth where relevant | The option that delivers the most trusted and timely support while improving successful re-engagement wins |
| Wellness Memory, Personalization Data, and User Data Collection | PM3 (AI Check-ins) | PM1, PM2, Privacy, Legal, and Design as needed | The option that creates the greatest customer value while maintaining trust, privacy, and strategic alignment wins |
| Roadmap Prioritization Across Onboarding, Retention, and AI | Core Product Lead | PM1, PM2, PM3, Engineering, Design, and relevant stakeholders | Prioritize the initiative that most advances quarterly OKRs and validates the post-acute retention strategy |
| Requests from Growth, Sales, Clinical, or Other External Teams | Core Product Lead | Relevant PMs and requesting stakeholders | Strategy and OKRs take precedence over local stakeholder goals or visibility |
| Quarterly Commitments, Funding, Headcount, and Strategic Direction | Head of Product / Product Leadership | Core Product Lead, PMs, and affected functional leaders | The option that best supports the company strategy, customer outcomes, and long-term business goals wins |
| Escalation: Conflict Between PMs Within the Team | Core Product Lead | PM1 (Onboarding), PM2 (Retention), PM3 (AI Check-ins), and any relevant Engineering or Design leads | The option that best advances KR1 (Confidence), KR2 (Proactive Action), or KR3 (Successful Re-engagement) while supporting the Playing to Win strategy wins. Timeframe: Decision made within 3 business days after escalation |
| Escalation: Conflict Between Product and Growth | Core Product Lead | Growth Lead, affected PM(s), Analytics, and Design as needed | Product strategy and OKRs take precedence over engagement, acquisition, or traffic goals. The option that best improves post-acute retention and resilience outcomes wins. Timeframe: Decision made within 3 business days after escalation |
| Escalation: Conflict Between Product and Engineering | Core Product Lead for product prioritization; Engineering Manager for technical implementation approach | Relevant PM(s), Engineering Lead, and Design if customer experience is affected | Product decides what outcome must be achieved; Engineering decides how it is implemented. If trade-offs are required, prioritize the option that delivers the greatest progress toward quarterly OKRs. Timeframe: Decision made within 3 business days after escalation |
| Escalation: Conflict Between Product and Clinical/Compliance Stakeholders | Core Product Lead for roadmap prioritization; Clinical or Compliance Lead for regulatory, safety, or clinical requirements | Relevant PM(s), Legal, Clinical, and Design as needed | Safety, legal, and regulatory requirements are non-negotiable. For non-mandatory requests, the option that best supports strategy and customer outcomes wins. Timeframe: Decision made within 3 business days after escalation |
| Escalation: Conflict Affecting Quarterly Commitments, Funding, or Headcount | Head of Product / Product Leadership Team | Core Product Lead, affected PMs, Engineering Leadership, and other impacted functional leaders | The option that best supports company strategy, customer outcomes, and long-term business goals wins. Timeframe: Decision made within 1 week after escalation |
| Escalations from outside the team | Core Product Lead |  |  |

> Our default: PMs collaboratively decide or defer to the correct PM; we escalate to the Core Product Lead when we cannot reach agreement.
